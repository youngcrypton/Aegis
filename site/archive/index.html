How it works

Watch, hedge, restore. Nothing is sold.

01

Watch

Sentinels read HyperCore order books and mark prices every few milliseconds against your trigger.

02

Hedge

When health crosses your threshold, AEGIS opens a short perp that offsets your exposure. Collateral is never sold.

03

Restore

Close the hedge when the market settles, or override it instantly from the dashboard.

Security

Built so the vault cannot withdraw.

Trade-only session keys

Your delegated key can place hedges and nothing else. Withdrawals are blocked at the contract level.

Slashable sentinel bonds

Every sentinel node stakes a minimum bond that is forfeited if it misses its service levels.

One-click override

Revoke Sentinel authority or close every hedge in a single action, at any time.
