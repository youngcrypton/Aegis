## Applying the Square Logo in GitBook (1-Minute Setup)

1. Open [GitBook App](https://app.gitbook.com/) and navigate to the **aegis** site.
2. Click **Customization** in the top navigation bar.
3. Under the **General** tab:
   * **Custom logo / Site Icon**: Upload **`gitbook-logo-square-180.png`** (or `gitbook-logo-square-transparent-180.png`).
   * **Icon / Favicon**: Upload **`gitbook-logo-square-512.png`**.
4. Under **Icons, colors, and themes**:
   * **Color Mode**: Select **Dark** (Ground: `#1C2238`, Raised: `#22403F`).
   * **Primary Color**: Enter `#5FD9BE` (the Aegis dark mode token `--accent: #5FD9BE`).
5. Click **Save** / **Publish changes**.
