How it works

Watch, hedge, restore. Nothing is sold.

01 · WATCH

Sentinels read the book

Sentinel nodes track HyperCore order books and mark prices against your trigger, checking continuously.

02 · HEDGE

A short offsets the drop

When health crosses your threshold, AEGIS opens a perpetual short sized to your hedge ratio. Your collateral stays put.

03 · RESTORE

Close when the market settles

Close the hedge automatically or override it in one action from the dashboard, at any time.

Security

Built so the vault cannot withdraw.

01

Trade-only session keys

Your delegated key can place hedges and nothing else. Withdrawals are blocked at the contract level.

02

Slashable sentinel bonds

Every sentinel node stakes a minimum bond that is forfeited if it misses its service levels.

03

One-click override

Revoke Sentinel authority or close every hedge in a single action, whenever you choose.
